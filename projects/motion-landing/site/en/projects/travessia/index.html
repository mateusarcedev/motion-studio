creative engineering bts

06 TÓPICOS

From pure mathematical Three.js procedural modeling to deterministic bit-by-bit multiformat mastering with MOTION Studio.

3D

Procedural Modeling & GLB

Without relying on external 3D desktop suites in headless environments, all meshes were procedurally coded with Three.js: 36 real corrugated panels, ISO twistlocks, 3-axle chassis, and gantry crane. Exported to lean binary GLB under 377 KB total.

Three.js r165 GLTFExporter BufferGeometry 377 KB
PBR

PBR Shaders & Lighting

Atmosphere governed by Industrial Cinematic Realism: contrasting maritime blue hour depth (#070A0F) with the utilitarian warmth of amber corten steel (#FF8A00). Wet asphalt with specular reflections and crisp cyan floodlights (#00F0FF).

MeshStandardMaterial ACESFilmic Roughness 0.15 PCFSoftShadow
CAM

Adaptive Multi-Viewport Camera

On narrow viewports (360px to 390px), standard camera framing would clip the ends of the 12-meter container. The SceneDirector controller continuously recalculates camera FOV and Z-axis distance in real time across any screen ratio.

Dynamic FOV Scale Factor Zero Overflow Touch >= 44px
CFR

Deterministic CFR 30 FPS Render

Decoupled from wall-clock time through MOTION Studio's headless frame driver. All 600 frames were rendered individually via Playwright Core and assembled with FFmpeg in exact Constant Frame Rate with zero dropped frames.

Playwright Core FFmpeg 8 yuv420p Bit-by-Bit CFR
QA

Automated Quality Audit

Comparative contact sheet generation for continuous visual inspection, automated horizontal overflow detection across 5 viewports (360px to 1920px), color contrast checks, and SHA-256 asset integrity validation.

Contact Sheet 5 Viewports Axe-Core Zero Console Err
ORQ

Multiformat Orchestration

A single conceptual briefing simultaneously powered the interactive 3D Web app, two master films (16:9 widescreen and 9:16 vertical), a 5-slide editorial carousel, and technical documentation, unified by tokens and visual memory.

06

transparency & technical notes

LIMITAÇÕES

Rigorous accounting of technical decisions, trade-offs, and actual project constraints without synthetic metrics.

No Commercial Audio Track

Master videos were rendered without audio (DEF-03). As an open-source project and studio showcase, unlicensed audio tracks were consciously excluded to maintain visual master integrity.

Blender-Free Procedural Pipeline

The absence of traditional DCC desktop software spurred mathematical reverse engineering of geometric meshes in native Three.js, delivering GLBs under 400 KB that load instantaneously in browsers.

CFR Determinism vs Real Time

While the interactive web experience relies on requestAnimationFrame with continuous lerp, master films were captured via discrete clock injection (1/30s), eradicating all CPU jitter.

Strict WebGL DPR Budgeting

WebGL Device Pixel Ratio was capped at 1.75x, preventing thermal throttling on ultra-high-DPI mobile screens and sustaining stable 60 FPS in interactive browsing.
