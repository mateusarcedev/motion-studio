Programmatic Motion Design

Decoupled 2D Virtual Camera

Strict separation between World Space (pan/zoom over diagrams) and Screen Space (fixed editorial titles with safe zones), preventing layout overlap.

World vs Screen Space Zero Collisions
04

Absolute Frame Rendering

Deterministic contract via `window.__onFrame__(n, 1800, 30)`. No wall clock or frame drop: frame N is mathematically identical across renders.

Deterministic Time 100% Reproducible
05

Headless Playwright Capture

Headless Chromium renders each frame at exact 1080×1920 viewport, capturing 1,800 PNG stills at high fidelity with zero jitter or tearing.

playwright-core Chromium 1248
